## License and treatment

Both source pages identify the photographs as available under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). That license permits downloading, copying, modifying, and distributing images in commercial and noncommercial projects without required attribution. It prohibits selling substantially unmodified photographs and building a competing image library. Attribution is included here and in the landing page's offline photo credits.

The files were delivered by Unsplash's image CDN as JPEGs, scaled to 2,800 pixels wide at quality 88. The page uses CSS cropping to fit each frame. No filters, color overlays, retouching, or generative edits are applied. The two images are not added to project data, backups, or the app's selectable demo-photo library.

The adjacent `manifest.json` records source URLs, dimensions, and SHA-256 hashes for verification. Source links require internet; the attribution, this license summary, and both photographs are available locally.
